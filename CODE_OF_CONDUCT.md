# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and inclusive environment for
everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Welcome questions and contributions from newcomers.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and acknowledge mistakes.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, trolling,
deliberate disruption, and the publication of private information without permission
are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and
other spaces where someone represents the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact options
on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/crimsonKn1ght). Do not open a
public issue containing sensitive personal information. Reports will be reviewed as
promptly and confidentially as practical. Project maintainers may edit or remove
content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
